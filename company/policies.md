# Policies

1. **High-value invoices.** Any vendor invoice above INR 1,00,000 must be approved by the Finance Manager before it is entered into the ERP.
2. **Vendor master changes.** Any change to vendor contact, remittance or bank details must be approved by the Finance Manager (fraud control).
3. **No duplicates.** Never enter the same vendor invoice twice.
4. **Data handling.** Do not send company data outside company systems. Never share credentials.
5. When an approval is denied, stop that action and report back; do not look for another way to do it.
