# Procedures

## Entering a vendor invoice (Accounts Payable)
1. Find the invoice email in Mail and open the attached invoice document; take values from the invoice document itself.
2. Check the ERP invoice list first. If the same vendor invoice number is already recorded, do not enter it again: report that it already exists.
3. Enter it under "Enter a new vendor invoice" with the vendor's invoice number, amount, currency, invoice date and due date.
   Put the source email subject in Notes.
4. Confirm the saved invoice by opening its ERP record. The ERP ID is the reference to give back to the requester.

## Updating vendor master data
Vendor contact, remittance and bank details are changed under ERP > Vendor master > Edit. The request must come from the
vendor's known contact address. Changes always need approval (see policies).

## Customer support triage
Every email in the Support folder becomes exactly one Helpdesk ticket with the customer's email, the email subject,
a priority and a category, and a one-line description.
- Priority High + category Outage: the service is down or unusable for a customer, or there is a security issue.
- Priority Medium + category Billing: questions about invoices, charges, renewals or billing dates.
- Priority Low + category Feature request: product suggestions. Anything else: Low + Other.
